# Code of conduct

i3 Academy is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. We
want everyone who takes part to be able to do so without harassment, whether they report a bug,
review a pull request or ask a question.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new or who write in a second language.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with arguments, and accept a decision once it is made.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Respect people's time: search before you ask, and keep reports reproducible.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone else's personal data, or data from an installation you do not
  run.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or intimidation, in public or in private.
- Discriminating remarks about a person's origin, gender, identity, age, disability, religion or
  any similar characteristic.
- Sexualised language or images.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeatedly disrupting a discussion after being asked to stop.

## Where it applies

In every space of the i3 Academy projects: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and
any other channel where someone acts on behalf of the project.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainers at **conduct@i3-innovation.com**. Only the maintainers read these reports,
and we keep them confidential. We answer every report. We may then take any action we find fitting,
from a private word to a permanent ban. A maintainer who is involved in a report takes no part in
handling it.
